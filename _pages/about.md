I am a sixth-year Ph.D. candidate in Economics at [Fordham University](https://www.fordham.edu/), advised by [Erick Rengifo](https://www.fordham.edu/academics/departments/economics/faculty/erick-w-rengifo/). I expect to graduate in May 2027 and will be on the job market in the 2026–27 cycle.

My research is on the financial stability consequences of digital money.

Before Fordham I received an M.S. in Economics from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://www.wisc.edu/) in 2021 and a B.A. in Economics from [Liaoning University](https://enweb.lnu.edu.cn/) in 2019.

I also maintain a [working paper monitor](https://weiyeecon.github.io/papers-monitor/) for digital assets and financial stability, updated every Monday.
