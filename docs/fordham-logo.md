# Fordham footer logo

The logo links to Fordham University from the lower-right corner of every public page. Desktop uses the horizontal primary logo at 280 pixels wide; mobile uses the stacked primary logo at 225 pixels wide. Both have 24 pixels of clear space. The light design displays maroon artwork on white; dark mode displays white artwork on Fordham maroon (`#7e072c`).

Source: [Fordham University Brand Standards — Logo](https://live.standards.site/fordham-university/logo), accessed October 4, 2026.

The horizontal maroon, stacked maroon, and horizontal white SVGs were downloaded from the artwork publicly displayed on that page. Their artwork is unchanged. The stacked white version changes only the original stacked SVG's maroon fill to the approved white color; its paths and proportions are unchanged.

These are the primary shield-and-wordmark logos. The university seal and athletics marks are not used. The logo follows the page's recommended minimum widths and has no effects, outlines, or altered proportions.
