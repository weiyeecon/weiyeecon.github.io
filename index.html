Ph.D. Candidate in Economics

I study digital money and financial stability, with a focus on stablecoins, central bank digital currencies, and tokenized real-world assets.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Fordham University, advised by Erick W. Rengifo. My dissertation, Essays on the Future of Finance, examines how new forms of money and financial infrastructure affect banking and risk.
